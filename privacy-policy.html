Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Welcome to Tasbih & Dhikr. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

1. Information We Collect

Tasbih & Dhikr does not require account registration and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number or other personal information to use its main features.

The app stores information such as counter values, Dhikr selections, favourites, progress, settings and activity history locally on your device using browser storage.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Information stored on your device is used to provide the app's features, including:

  • Dhikr and Tasbih counting.
  • Saving your counter progress.
  • Managing favourite Dhikr.
  • Remembering your app settings.
  • Maintaining your activity history.
  • Supporting backup and restore features.

3. Local Storage and Backup

Your counter data and preferences are stored locally on your device. The app does not currently provide an online account or cloud synchronisation service.

If you use the Backup feature, a backup file containing your app data may be saved to your device. You control where you store or share that file.

4. Data Sharing

The current version of Tasbih & Dhikr does not include an advertising SDK or analytics SDK. We do not sell your personal information.

If third-party services are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the relevant services and their data practices.

5. Advertising

The current version of the app does not display third-party advertisements and does not currently use Google AdMob.

If advertising services such as Google AdMob are added in a future version, this section and the Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the applicable advertising and data practices.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information saved locally remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

You can also use the app's data deletion feature to delete the data stored by the app. Please export a backup first if you want to keep your information.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is a general-purpose Dhikr counter. It does not knowingly request personal information from children.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the app and its data. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
